A Note to Our Closest Friends & Family

An Intimate Evening Together

You are receiving this page because you mean the world to us. To celebrate five years of marriage, we have reserved the candlelit Private Dining Room at Osteria Via Stato for an intimate, cozy evening shared exclusively with our closest circle of family and friends.

Evening Details

What to Expect

The evening begins promptly at 6:00 PM. We kindly suggest arriving by 5:45 PM so everyone is settled in before we begin.

5:45 PM

Recommended Arrival

Check in at the host stand to be escorted to the Private Dining Room, find your seat, and settle in before we begin.

6:00 PM

Ceremony

Our vow renewal begins promptly at six as we gather together to celebrate five years of love, growth, and marriage.

6:30 PM

Cake Cutting

We raise a glass and cut our celebration cake together as the evening transitions into cocktails and celebration.

6:45 PM

Dinner & Celebration

An intimate, candlelit Italian dinner with an open bar, flowing cocktails, good vibes, and plenty of celebrating into the night—come ready to eat, drink, and party with our favorite people.

Just bring your best energy—we've taken care of the rest.
Unplugged Ceremony

No Cell Phones During the Vows

We invite you to be fully present in the room with us during this deeply personal moment. We kindly ask that all cell phones, cameras, and smartwatches be silenced and tucked away throughout the vow renewal ceremony.

Our professional photographer will be capturing every laugh, tear, and reaction—and we promise to share the entire gallery with everyone afterward. Once dinner and cocktails begin, feel free to snap away!
